## 3. Brand Asset Specifications & Palette

### Vector Geometry Specs
- **Outer Document Boundary**: 34×44 unit rectangle with `rx="2"` or `rx="2.5"` corner radius.
- **Golden Ratio Grid Coordinates**: Horizontal division line at `y=18` (dashed `2 2`), vertical division line at `x=20` (dashed `2 2`).
- **Focal Node**: Circle centered at `(20, 18)` with radius `r=3.5` (or `r=4` on icon), filled with vermillion gradient.
- **Geometric Corner Badge**: Vector path `M2,2 L14,2 C14,14 2,14 2,14 Z` filling the top-left corner quadrant.

### Color Tokens
- **Dark Neutral (Frame/Text)**: `#18181B` (Zinc 900)
- **Light Neutral (Dark Mode Frame/Text)**: `#F4F4F5` (Zinc 100)
- **Vermillion Red Gradient Start**: `#EF4444` (Red 500)
- **Vermillion Red Gradient End**: `#B91C1C` (Red 700)
- **Wordmark Accent**: `#DC2626` (Red 600) / `#EF4444` (Red 500 in dark mode)
- **Grid Guides (Light)**: `#A1A1AA` (Zinc 400)
- **Grid Guides (Dark)**: `#71717A` (Zinc 500)
- **Tagline Text**: `#71717A` (Zinc 500) / `#A1A1AA` (Zinc 400 in dark mode)

### Typography
- **Primary Serif Wordmark**: `'Instrument Serif', Georgia, serif` (font-size 28pt, weight 400, letter-spacing `-0.01em`)
- **Wordmark Connector ("to")**: `'Geist', 'Inter', -apple-system, sans-serif` (font-size 18pt, weight 300)

## 4. UI/UX Design Guidance & Independence Note

**Important Boundary Note:**
The selection of Concept 3 (Golden Folio) applies to the **brand logo only**. The application UI is **not** required to adopt a traditional editorial layout or extensive serif styling.

The web application should possess a modern, creative SaaS software identity (clean visual hierarchy, high affordance, glassmorphism accents, ergonomic controls, responsive layouts) while cleanly displaying the Golden Folio mark.

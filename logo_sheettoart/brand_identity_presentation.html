Design Rationale

The 2×2 grid directly establishes the product's origin (data tables and tabular cells). The top-right cell breaks free and elevates into a vibrant diamond facet, visually depicting the exact moment data is transformed into a crafted document.

Strengths & Considerations

Strengths: Highly intuitive metaphor, fits modern developer/creator SaaS aesthetics, flawless 16px favicon reduction.
Consideration: Inner focal circle must be suppressed at micro-sizes to preserve silhouette clarity.

Design Rationale

Unifies the two initials (S for Sheet, A for Art) into a single continuous kinetic gesture. The flowing curved baseline represents raw tabular data streams, resolving into the sharp geometric apex of an architectural document.

Strengths & Considerations

Strengths: Highly distinctive tech hallmark (like Linear or Figma), memorable brand asset, great for merchandise/stickers.
Consideration: Requires optical stroke boosting when rendered at 16px to maintain separation between lines.

Design Rationale

Draws directly on Tschichold's golden canon and Swiss editorial architecture. The folio boundary and precision grid guide crosshair embody mathematical typesetting, paired with an optical serif wordmark reflecting publication-grade prestige.

Strengths & Considerations

Strengths: Highest editorial authority, exudes quality and executive poise, unmatched elegance on physical stationery.
Consideration: Editorial serif leans slightly more literary/classic than pure high-velocity dev SaaS.
